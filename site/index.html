A New York Story

A cozy lowpoly life-sim for the billions of people who have never been to New York — but have always felt it.

▶ Play in the browser View on GitHub

The fantasy

You arrive with $87 and a duffel bag. A bodega owner named Rosa takes pity on you. You get a cart. The city starts teaching you — patiently, warmly, one order and one postcard at a time.

For never-visitors

Zero NYC knowledge assumed. The city itself is the tutorial. No quizzes. No gatekeeping.

Wonder first

Every session has a designed “I wish I could see that” moment. The game composes your memories for you.

Postcard beauty

Lowpoly, golden-hour, vertical-first. Every frame readable in one second of scrolling.

Four days are alive — in your browser

Day 1 through Day 4 play end to end at the live build. One block of Manhattan, grown by hand: brownstones with stoops, a bodega, a subway plaza, street trees, pigeons with opinions. Day 4 even has an afternoon now. And the block has a sound — a quiet city, synthesized from scratch, with a mute button that means it.

Day 3 — A Place at the Corner

Rosa brings the news to YOUR cart. Four real orders. The kid comes down from his window — and takes the stool.

Day 4, afternoon — The Block Opens Up

Sunrise Laundry pulls its shutter up. Sal washes the mango-stained apron — first wash free. A city notice by the crosswalk sells you the Mobile Food Vending Permit ($10, and it asks twice), and a receipt goes up on the cart.

The block, grown by hand

Coral, teal, plum doors. Stoops with railings. Tree pits. The street reads as a street, not a grid.

Day 4 — Say Hi

A stranger with a duffel bag stands where the story started. You make her a regular — on the house — and the Map changes hands. The lamp next door finally stays on.

Every session ends with a postcard the game composes itself — real capture, real caption
