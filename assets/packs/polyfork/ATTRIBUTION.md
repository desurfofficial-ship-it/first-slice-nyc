# Polyfork NYC City Kit — the curated pack (the streets wave)

Source: https://polyfork.dev/kit/nyc-city-kit-fbca88 (the kit, 82 parts,
status: published). Licence: the free kit's mesh files (GLB) are
**CC BY 4.0** — `credit` per the API's field, e.g.
"Bike Rack by Polyfork (https://polyfork.dev/)". No `.mjs` modules and no
HD editions are included (the licence's redistribution terms).

## What ships here

- `glb/<id>.glb` — the ten parts the game wears (the extraction's source;
  the byte-truth of the committed vertex data in
  `web-three/src/world/polyforkData.ts`, generated by
  `tools3d/polyfork/extract_polyfork.py`).
- `meta/meta-<id>.json` — the kit API's own metadata for each part
  (title, triangles, size_m, palette, has_night, credit) — provenance.
