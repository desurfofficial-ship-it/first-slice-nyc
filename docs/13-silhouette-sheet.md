# FIRST SLICE — 20 Hero Prop Silhouette Sheet

**Purpose:** Guarantee that the most important objects in the game remain readable at a glance, even at very small sizes. This is a direct enforcement of the **1-Second Law**.

**Test standard:** Every prop below must be identifiable as a pure black silhouette on a mid-grey background at **64×64 pixels**.

---

## How to use this sheet

1. Model or draw each prop with strong, simplified forms.
2. Create a pure black silhouette version.
3. Scale it to 64×64 px.
4. Show it to someone who has never seen the game for 2 seconds.
5. If they cannot name it correctly, revise the proportions (usually make the distinctive part bigger or simpler).

---

## The 20 Hero Props

| # | Prop | Distinctive silhouette features | Notes |
|---|------|----------------------------------|-------|
| 1 | **Coffee / Food Cart** | Rectangular body + oversized awning + visible wheels | Must read as “small business on wheels.” Awning is the hero shape. |
| 2 | **Yellow Taxi** | Classic crown / long body + roof light | Instantly “New York.” Keep the roof light readable. |
| 3 | **Water Tower** | Cylindrical tank on tall legs | One of the strongest NYC skyline signatures. |
| 4 | **Fire Escape** | Zig-zag stairs + landings + vertical ladders | Should feel like outdoor jewelry on the building. |
| 5 | **Bodega Awning** | Scalloped or straight rectangular canopy sticking out | Strong horizontal shape. |
| 6 | **Neon OPEN sign** | Simple rectangular or slightly arched sign | Magic punctuation — used sparingly. |
| 7 | **Produce Crate Stack** | Stacked rectangular boxes, slightly irregular | Suggests life and abundance. |
| 8 | **Oversized Bagel** | Large ring shape | Playfully bigger than real life. |
| 9 | **Pizza Slice (folded)** | Triangular slice with a clear fold | The fold is part of the identity. |
| 10 | **Halal Cart Umbrella** | Large umbrella shape above a cart | Instant street-food read. |
| 11 | **Brownstone Stoop** | Wide steps + side rails + doorway | Outdoor living room. |
| 12 | **Pigeon** | Round body + small head + attitude | Slightly puffed, never cute-cartoon. |
| 13 | **Subway Entrance** | Stairs going down + globe lights or simple railing | Should feel like a portal. |
| 14 | **MTA Roundel (simplified)** | Circle with simple letter/number | Keep extremely readable. |
| 15 | **Steam Column** | Soft vertical rising shape | Must still read even when semi-transparent. |
| 16 | **String Lights** | Horizontal or draped line of small bulbs | Adds warmth and scale. |
| 17 | **Folding Chair** | Simple chair profile | Stoop / rooftop furniture. |
| 18 | **Paper Coffee Cup** | Cylindrical cup + sleeve + lid | The everyday object of the game. |
| 19 | **Map (folded paper)** | Rectangular folded sheet, slightly imperfect | Should feel handheld and personal. |
| 20 | **Single Lit Window** | Warm rectangular glow in a dark facade | The emotional progress indicator. |
