## 2. Day 1 Postcards (6)

All captions ≤ 2 sentences. One wonder beat each. Written so a never-visitor would want to keep them.

### Postcard 1 — Times Square (Wow Walk)
**Moment:** Emerging from the subway into golden-hour Times Square as the screens sync.
**Caption:**  
*Times Square — 300,000 people cross here every day.*  
*Today, so did you.*

### Postcard 2 — First Rush
**Moment:** End of the five-order rush at Rosa's, light slanting through the window.
**Caption:**  
*Five orders. One apron that still smelled like mangoes.*  
*The city let me keep up.*

### Postcard 3 — Water Towers
**Moment:** Looking up from the corner for the first time.
**Caption:**  
*The buildings wear wooden hats.*  
*I don't know why yet. I like them.*

### Postcard 4 — The Fold
**Moment:** First proper slice or bagel, folding it while walking.
**Caption:**  
*Folding it is part of the recipe.*  
*Grease on the napkin is proof you were here.*

### Postcard 5 — Your Corner
**Moment:** Standing in front of the newly named cart at golden hour.
**Caption:**  
*[Cart Name] — Day 1.*  
*It was ugly. So was Rosa's, once. It's mine now.*

### Postcard 6 — Day 1 Close (Skyline + Gold Window)
**Moment:** Fire-escape dusk, one window flickers then holds gold.
**Caption:**  
*Day 1. $87 → $112.*  
*Met: Rosa. Idris. Learned: the usual is a tiny love letter. The regulars are waiting.*
