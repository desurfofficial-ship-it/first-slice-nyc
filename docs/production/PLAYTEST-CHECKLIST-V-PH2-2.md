## After the session — log, don't interpret

Record in the register's V-PH2-2 row (or an attachment it points to):

- who played, when, on what device/browser, for how long;
- which of the six capabilities were seen (the ticks above, verbatim — no paraphrase into "passed");
- anything the player did that the game did not expect (observation only — the playtest-analysis law separates observation / interpretation / recommendation, and invents no numbers beyond the data);
- the session's own words about the block, quoted, if the player offers them.

**The honesty law:** a session that ends early or skips a capability is LOGGED as such — the variance stays open until the law's own bar is met. The automated substitutes stay green either way; they were never the human half.
