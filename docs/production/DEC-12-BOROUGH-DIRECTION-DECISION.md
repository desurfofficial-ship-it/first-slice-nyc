## 1. The ruling's terms (the owner's own structure, recorded verbatim in substance)

1. **Alder Heights is preserved as the foundation and first fully developed district.** Everything built stands untouched — 81 building rows, 21 interiors, 7 parks, 23 anchors, every probe and ledger row. No canon row is edited, renamed, or renumbered to make room (the registry's append-only id law holds).
2. **One additional district enters the design frame as a future chapter setting.** Expansion must be meaningful: a new neighbourhood identity, buildings, businesses, residents, activities, stories and environmental character — at the existing quality, interaction density and simulation standards (purpose + interaction + books per place; the Phase-6 machinery opens its books; the probe fleet walks it).
3. **Pricing before building.** The rendering and performance implications are priced before any geometry — the first honest deliverable of the design frame is the design package + first priced-wave draft, with the draw-budget question answered by numbers read from this project's own ledger (the shipped city runs 190 against the < 200 probe budget; the scoping study §3's finding — a second district at block density does not fit the current budget — stands until a DEC-2-class ruling prices the two-live-districts budget).
4. **No borough geometry or expansion implementation without the required fresh owner approval under §8.** The design package and its first priced wave are the gate's input; the gate is the owner's, fresh, on that input (the scoping study §5 binding law 1 — "this study is not that gate" — now extended: this ruling is not that gate either).
5. **The five-borough Passport (§0 pillar 6) stays a possible long-term ambition — not an immediate production commitment.** No Passport progression meta-system is designed, scheduled, or built; Option C remains the study's own "strictly larger" program for a future owner conversation.
6. **The identity is not narrowed.** The decision explicitly reconciles the wording that described FIRST SLICE as merely an open-block life simulation with the broader open-world New York simulation direction: **FIRST SLICE is an open-world New York life simulation, delivered chapter by chapter** — the shipped playable world is its first district (Alder Heights), simulated deeply enough to feel like a city, and the world grows by chapters under §8's gates. "Open block" describes today's build state, never the genre ceiling. AGENTS.md §0's direction line, the roadmap §1 reconciliation and the README tagline are re-worded this wave to say exactly this; the Roam law, the module fences and the scope guards all stand unchanged.
