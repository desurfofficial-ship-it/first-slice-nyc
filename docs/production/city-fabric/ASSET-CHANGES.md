The registry's `bounds.skylineCards` documented four vanishing-point planes
that existed only as documentation. They are now real buildings — **one merged
fog-exempt indexed vc mesh (`SkylineFabric`, 20,874 verts) + one InstancedMesh
of lit panes (`SkylineFabricWindows`, 142 panes) sharing the shipped skyline
window material** (the dusk tween dresses them with the towers', for free):

| Row | Line | Character |
|---|---|---|
| `SKY-W` | x = −110 | The Juniper Avenue vanishing point: masonry/limestone mid-rise with a canyon-height boost at the Kestrel sightline |
| `SKY-E` | x = +110 | The Sycamore Avenue vanishing point (mirror profile) |
| `SKY-S` | z = −88 | The downtown cluster: taller (up to ~55 m), 45% glass, commercial lofts, industrial boxes with loading-door ribs |
| `SKY-N` | z = +104 | A depth row behind the shipped tower row (capped at 30 m so the shipped row keeps its silhouette) |

Variation delivered per the brief: heights 10–55 m, widths 9–18 m, five style
families (pre-war masonry / limestone / commercial loft / industrial / glass),
cornice lips on every mass break, 1–2 upper setbacks on tall masses, whole
window-bay cells on masonry faces (dark cells + 34% lit), glass spandrel
bands every ~4 m, rooftop tanks/bulkheads/masts per building.
