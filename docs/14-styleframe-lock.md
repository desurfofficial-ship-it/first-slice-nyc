## Locked Styleframes

### Styleframe A — Golden-Hour Corner
**File:** [`assets/styleframes/styleframe-a-golden-hour-corner.jpg`](../assets/styleframes/styleframe-a-golden-hour-corner.jpg)

**Decision:** **LOCKED** as the primary daytime / cart reference.

**Why:**
- Strongest overall 1-Second Law performance
- Clear hero cart
- Correct warm palette (brick, taxi yellow, golden-hour amber, steam)
- Good sense of place and scale
- Directly useful for the most common Day 1 view

**Follow-up notes:**
- Cart design in this frame is the preferred direction going forward
- String lights, steam, and taxi language should be matched in production

### Styleframe B — Dusk Fire Escape + Gold Window
**File:** [`assets/styleframes/styleframe-b-dusk-fire-escape.jpg`](../assets/styleframes/styleframe-b-dusk-fire-escape.jpg)

**Decision:** **LOCKED** as the emotional / dusk / Skyline of Lit Windows reference.

**Why:**
- Best emotional read of the three
- Fire-escape green + dusk plum palette is correct
- Single gold window reads clearly and supports the core fantasy
- Strong vertical composition
- Directly supports the final Day 1 moment

**Follow-up notes:**
- This is the target mood for the fire-escape sequence and the first gold window
- Character proportions here are acceptable for lowpoly direction

### Styleframe C — First Postcard Composition
**File:** [`assets/styleframes/styleframe-c-first-postcard.jpg`](../assets/styleframes/styleframe-c-first-postcard.jpg)

**Decision:** **LOCKED as postcard framing reference**, with one required consistency fix.

**Why:**
- Deckled paper border is excellent and should be treated as production target
- Composition and caption space work well
- Skyline + taxi + steam support the “keepsake” feeling

**Required follow-up:**
- The cart in Styleframe C (green body + scalloped yellow awning) is **not** the production cart.
- Future postcard frames and marketing images must use the cart language established in **Styleframe A**.
- The border treatment and overall postcard framing from C remain locked.
