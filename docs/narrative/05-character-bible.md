## 1. Cast design rules (binding)

1. **No quest-vending.** No character exists to hand out tasks. Every major has income, obligations, and pleasures that generate behavior with or without the player (doc 07 §7 specifies each one's off-screen tick).
2. **Refusal rights.** Any character may refuse, delay, misremember, or counter-offer. Refusals are written as character, not as gates.
3. **Independent error.** Each major makes at least one significant mistake per chapter that is *theirs* — not caused by, not fixed by, the protagonist.
4. **Ensemble web.** Information travels by the gossip physics of doc 04 §4. Nobody knows something implausibly; nobody stays ignorant of something shouted from their stoop.
5. **Representation care.** The cast's cultures are written from the inside (food, worship, money habits, humor) and never as accent costumes. No character is a stereotype, a saint, or a lesson. Where the bible touches real-world practice (carting industry history, public-housing mechanics, halal schedules), it stays restrained and credible; uncertain details are flagged in doc 15 rather than invented.

## 2. Major cast

### 2.1 Eddie Carrasco — "Big Eddie," 61 · Corner Slice Pizza

**Background.** Washington Heights-born, Dominican family, started washing pans at the old slice joint at 19, bought the place (and the name) from its founding Greek owners in Y-37. Widower (Elena, Y-9). Son Junior moved to Charlotte; Eddie stayed with the oven. Diabetic, knees gone, hands still sure.
**Personality.** Gruff economy: short declaratives, food metaphors, tests people with small cruelties before he feeds them. Generous in ways he'd deny under oath. Reads a person's character by how they treat the mop.
**Wants.** To pass the shop on whole — not sold, not gentrified, *whole*. To be visited by his son without it feeling like surrender.
**Fears.** The body quitting before the lease does; dying and the shop dying the same week.
**Weaknesses.** Pride that mistakes stubbornness for strength; hides symptoms until they park him; trusts too slowly and then completely.
**Money.** The building isn't his — Doris Hollis is his landlord of 37 years, friend price. The *business* is his: ~$9K/mo gross, thin margins, a renewals war he's losing politely.
**Secrets.** Knows exactly what Craig is; says nothing because Doris once co-signed his own loan. Keeps a cassette of Elena singing; has never played it for anyone.
**Routines.** In at 5:10, dough by 5:40, the first slice cut at 6:55 for whoever opened. Dominos at Corner Park on Sundays — Tito's table, loses on purpose when Tito's had bad news. Mass at the Ascension once a month, stands in the back.
**With the protagonist.** Hired him off a listing because "he looked hungry in the useful way." Sees the son-shaped hole and fills it without naming it. → arc: doc 07 §2.1.

### 2.2 Rosa Delgado — 57 · Rosa's Deli & Grocery

**Background.** Three decades behind neighborhood counters; the gold rooftop billboard is her late husband's last practical joke and best investment. Opened the deli twenty-three years ago with $400 and a borrowed griddle ("Nineteen years ago I made eleven" — her first day's take, a story she still tells).
**Personality.** The block's court of appeals. Dry, warm, unhurryable. Teaches by letting you do it wrong once. Never raises her voice; lowers it.
**Wants.** The block to keep working: the kid fed, the newcomers taught, the old ones visited. A successor who isn't a franchise.
**Fears.** The block's kindness becoming a luxury good. Her own tombstone-to-be reading "She was nice" instead of "She was load-bearing."
**Weaknesses.** Cannot fire anyone; carries four employees she doesn't need. Sees everything and mistakes seeing for fixing.
**Money.** Owns her leasehold building through a family LLC — but the LLC owes the Hollis estate money by C5 (Doris lent her the down payment, Y-11; nothing is written down; everything is remembered).
**Secrets.** The cart she lends Leo was her first business. The anniversary party (C2) is the one day a year she isn't the strong one.
**Routines.** Opens at 6 with the radio on the AM news; feeds the terminal's overnighters at 4:30 a.m. unadvertised; Thursday dominoes; keeps every IOU in a cigar box and redeems none.
**With the protagonist.** His first teacher and his clearest mirror; she spots the condition's shape before anyone ("You write things down like a man who's been robbed") and never asks. → doc 07 §2.2.

### 2.3 Yolanda "Yoli" Ferreira — 34 · organizer, Juniper Mutual Aid

**Background.** Third-generation Alder Heights; Tito's daughter; Ernie's niece (doesn't know the 1994 story yet). SUNY degree, community-board apprenticeship, then quit the formal track when the rezoning rumor started. Runs JMA out of a papered-window storefront.
**Personality.** Precise, weaponized-polite, allergic to charm. Makes lists; wins with them. The only person in the story who reads footnotes for fun.
**Wants.** A neighborhood that keeps its people. Specifically: a community land trust before the crane arrives — the Compact's ghost, refought and won.
**Fears.** Becoming her uncle: reasonable at the dinner table, fatal at the signature line.
**Weaknesses.** Purity as armor; mistakes refusal for principle twice (C2's all-or-nothing compact strategy costs the Oktays everything); cannot accept help without itemizing it.
**Money.** $2,400/mo stipend (two grants, both expiring), no savings, student loans in forbearance.
**Secrets.** Applied for — and turned down — a Corvus "community liaison" salary in Y-3. The letter is in her desk.
**Routines.** Garden at 6:30; flyer rounds after the 7:40 platform crowd; board meetings twice a month; Sunday dinner at Tito's, non-negotiable.
**With the protagonist.** Reads his charm as a tool, then reads *him* as a tool using tools. The story's conscience who is also, in C2, its cautionary tale. → doc 07 §2.3.

### 2.4 Kevin Okafor — "Kev," 22 · counter, then protégé

**Background.** The Okafor house, Gable Row 1. Community college (two semesters, IT) ended by money; delivery apps and the counter at Corner Slice. Mother Grace cleans homes; father Raymond is MTA track maintenance. The Gable Garden is the family's plot since the Compact era.
**Personality.** Earnest, funny online, fluent in the internet's dialects; face-to-face he apologizes for existing. Unbearably loyal to anyone who says he's good at something.
**Wants.** To matter somewhere that isn't a group chat. To move his mother's name off the "past due" column.
**Fears.** Being someone's useful idiot and finding out in public.
**Weaknesses.** Believes authority figures' drafts of him. Signs things.
**Money.** $15/hr at the shop + apps; sends Grace $200/mo; hides it badly.
**Secrets.** Screenshots everything — every promise, every text, "in case." The habit will arm the C5 cascade.
**Routines.** Streetball at the half-court Thursdays (Darnell's crowd); streaming co-op with his cousin until 2 a.m.; church with Grace on first Sundays.
**With the protagonist.** The kid Leo could have been if someone had handed him a ladder. The protégé, the Betrayal II victim, and — doc 08 — the instrument of the fall. → doc 07 §2.4.

### 2.5 June Park — 28 · co-owner, Halcyon (the café shell, 6 Kestrel East)

**Background.** Queens-raised, line cook at seven years of kitchens, inherited $190K at 26 when her grandmother died; opened Halcyon (café by day, three-nights-a-week wine bar) with Fitz. Five-year lease in a Hollis building — the same stack of buildings as Corner Slice, a fact she and Leo will both learn the hard way.
**Personality.** Dry, sensory, exact; calls people on specifics ("You said Tuesday."). Workaholic with a gardener's patience. Reads people through their hands — what they do with them while talking.
**Wants.** A place that's hers on paper AND in practice; to cook one perfect thing a day; a partner who doesn't need managing.
**Fears.** Becoming a landlord story; inheriting her grandmother's last decade (stubborn, alone, beloved).
**Weaknesses.** Believes honesty is a floor, not a favor — the diary discovery nearly ends her; conflict-avoidant about money with friends.
**Money.** The build-out ate $210K; Halcyon breaks even in C3, profits modestly after; the lease renewal is her sword of Damocles.
**Secrets.** Kept $30K of the inheritance in an envelope for her brother, who is not doing well in Flushing.
**Routines.** Bakes at 4:30; farmer's market Saturdays; pool at the Y on Mondays; reads permit filings for fun now, which she hates.
**With the protagonist.** Slow, real romance (C2 onward). Discovers the diary in C4 (doc 03 K3). Her conditions for what happens next are the hardest lines in doc 07. → doc 07 §2.5.

### 2.6 Marisol Quintero — 45 · principal, Corvus Group

**Background.** Sunset Park; parents cleaned offices; NYU at night; city planning department at 24, developer at 31, her own shop at 38. Juniper Commons is the project she wants to be judged by.
**Personality.** Fluent in two languages of power and switches between them mid-sentence. Warm the way a render is warm. Genuinely believes density is justice and opposition is nostalgia with a petition.
**Wants.** Juniper Commons done right, on time, with her name on the bronze plaque *and* on the record of the 220 affordable units she'll fight the city to fund.
**Fears.** Being her father's employer story: the boss who laid off a whole floor the year she got the corner office.
**Weaknesses.** Contempt she keeps surgically hidden; the belief that everyone has a price, which is self-fulfilling; underestimates old women with paper.
**Money.** Real money; also real leverage problems — construction debt, a rate environment that punishes delay, an anchor tenant (the food hall) she needs to be *beloved*.
**Secrets.** The Wexler method: he finds the violation, she never asks how. She knows about SaniHaul's interest in her site and has decided to know it less.
**Routines.** 5 a.m. gym; reads every community-board minute herself; eats at the block's restaurants when she visits — visibly, deliberately, and it still doesn't work.
**With the protagonist.** She recognizes the hunger because she grew up next to it. Recruiter, mirror, and — in C4 — his accomplice of convenience. Offers him the food hall because he is perfect for it and perfect *for her*. → doc 07 §2.6.

### 2.7 Dane Wexler — 38 · Corvus "expediter"

**Background.** Queens; DOB examiner for nine years until a consulting firm noticed him. Does what the law permits and reports what the law requires, in the order that serves the client.
**Personality.** Pleasant, unhurried, remembers everyone's children's names. Never lies; selects.
**Wants.** The firm partnership. A boat, eventually, ironically.
**Fears.** Nothing he'd name; he's read enough forfeiture files to know how fast "nothing" can be reclassified.
**Weaknesses.** Process — his whole power is procedural, and the Compact papers (doc 06 C5) attack him procedurally.
**Secrets.** SaniHaul pays him a "consulting" stipend via a firm he owns with his brother-in-law.
**With the protagonist.** Professional respect, zero sentiment. Handles Leo the way you handle a permit: process until it's cheaper to buy.

### 2.8 Gino Trabucco — 57 · "waste logistics," SaniHaul of New York

**Background.** Bensonhurst; twenty years in the family carting business back when it had a different name and worse manners; reads Papini and naval history; tailors well.
**Personality.** Disgustingly reasonable. Threatens nobody, ever; makes it clear that problems are *arrangeable*. Feeds people; it is not a trick, and it is also a trick.
**Wants.** The Works demolition and hauling contracts; the church parking lot (staging); a world where his industry's history stays unspoken.
**Fears.** Subpoenas; his daughter's future in-laws reading the files; being spoken about with the wrong past tense.
**Weaknesses.** Old-world bookkeeping; loyalties he can't modernize; underestimates "civic" people until the exact moment he doesn't.
**Secrets.** Bought Craig Hollis's paper in C4 at 60 cents. Calls it "helping a neighbor."
**With the protagonist.** Two lunches in C3–C4, both paid for, both remembered. Leo's favors-from-Bert canon lives here (doc 08 §4): the DOB violation that disappeared, and the one that didn't.

### 2.9 Craig Hollis — 52 · the landlord's son

**Background.** Grew up with eleven buildings and one job: don't lose them. Lost everything in Florida instead (a kayak-rental franchise, don't ask). Back two years, managing Doris's portfolio "until she's ready to rest."
**Personality.** Apology-forward, grievance-deep. Charms servers, resents owners. The most dangerous kind of weak: weak with receipts.
**Wants.** His own life back at someone else's expense; to be respected *just once* without a "yet."
**Fears.** Doris's ledger — the other kind, the moral one — and what it says about him.
**Weaknesses.** SaniHaul's paper; contempt for tenants he'd die before showing; cannot believe the family buildings are worth more than his dignity.
**Secrets.** Signed a personal guarantee in Florida that survives to this day.
**With the protagonist.** Sees a younger version of the thing he despises in himself — which is why he underestimates Leo exactly once.

### 2.10 Doris Hollis — 78 · matriarch

**Background.** Married into the buildings in 1969; ran them herself from 1991. Friend prices were her husband's idea; keeping them was her spine. Dialysis Tuesdays and Fridays.
**Personality.** Old-school forms of address ("young man"), total recall for every lease, every favor, every funeral. Says the true thing last, at the door.
**Wants.** To leave the buildings *right* — priced fairly, kept whole, not sold in a bag.
**Fears.** Craig with the checkbook; the buildings outliving her judgment.
**Weaknesses.** Boiler debt, roof debt, and a heart the doctors keep recalibrating.
**Secrets.** Kept Ernie Ferreira's 1994 letter's existence to herself for thirty years (Tito's business, but she saw the vote).
**With the protagonist.** Prices him accurately within ninety seconds and likes him anyway, which is worse. Her death (C5) is the conflict's hinge. → doc 07 §2.10.

### 2.11 Priya Nair — 34 · K-line conductor

**Background.** Eleven years on the K; raised in Jersey; the GDD's "Subway University" is her doing — she runs unofficial station-history walks on days off. Knows the system the way farmers know weather.
**Personality.** Three speeds: conductor-announcer (crisp), colleague (dry), friend (merciless). Collects station stamps; finishing the system is her decade-long album.
**Wants.** Every station; a book about the underground nobody has asked her to write; for the neighborhood's kids to know the city is *theirs*.
**Fears.** The line being "optimized" into a shuttle; becoming a person whose best decade was a uniform.
**Weaknesses.** Neutral till it's cowardice — she sees everything and reports selectively, a policy that costs her in C5 when her silence about Craig's schedule-riding is read as collaboration.
**Secrets.** She knew the Oktays' eviction was coming eleven days early (the paperwork moves through the system where she lives) and said nothing, because it wasn't hers to say. It was.
**With the protagonist.** Early friend, transit-savant, the source of the "dominoes" warning ("This block is a hand of tiles. Learn which one you are."). → doc 07 §2.11.

### 2.12 Deshaun Carter — 27 · EMT, Station 40; "DJ Deep Freeze"

**Background.** Bronx-raised, third-generation city worker; EMT five years, studying for the paramedic exam (fails it once, in C3 — on his own, unrelated to the plot, which is the point). Darnell's older cousin. Decks in his room, weddings on weekends.
**Personality.** Gallows humor with a soft floor; competence as affection; arrives *with* the plan, not to discuss one.
**Wants.** The patch (paramedic), his mother's garage converted to a studio, one job where he isn't the only one who knows the protocol.
**Fears.** Burnout becoming his whole personality; the day he's slow on a call.
**Weaknesses.** Fixes people instead of telling them the truth; lends money he doesn't have.
**Money.** $48K + OT; Verna (his mother) is one of Leo's investors — $2,300, her tax refund.
**With the protagonist.** First friend in New York — met on Day 1 when Leo's panic attack got mistaken for a medical one on the platform (doc 11). The friendship is the story's cleanest thing, which is why the story tests it with Verna's money. → doc 07 §2.12.

### 2.13 Mr. Idris Warsame — 63 · night orderly, Harlem Hospital

**Background.** Somalia → Cairo → the Bronx in the 80s; thirty years of nights. The shop's 6:55 customer — the first slice ritual's beneficiary more often than not ("Coffee. Regular. I'll be back tomorrow" is Corner Slice's version — tea, two sugars, the corner stool).
**Personality.** Economical, precise kindness; faith worn comfortably. The neighborhood's memory of what work is *for*.
**Wants.** His granddaughter Amina through nursing school; the fajr walk to Masjid an-Nur to stay quiet and safe; to be useful until the last week.
**Fears.** Being a burden; the block forgetting the night shift exists.
**Weaknesses.** Under-bargains for himself always; belief that institutions hear you if you're polite.
**Money.** Pension + nights; invests **$600** in the Juniper Club because Leo asked him with respect, which is the tragedy (doc 09).
**With the protagonist.** The quiet conscience: never lectures, occasionally states a sentence that takes Leo a chapter to understand.

### 2.14 Deniz Oktay — 24 · Kestrel Hardware's daughter

**Background.** Born above the hardware store; City College night school (accounting) while working the register. Her parents Mehmet and Ayşe lose the store in C2 (doc 06) — the lease was Corvus-assembled, the rent doubled, the fight lost politely.
**Personality.** Cold is what her anger looks like from outside; inside it's arithmetic. Keeps the receipts — literal, exhaustive — because her father taught her every disagreement is a document.
**Wants.** The store's story to be *on the record* somewhere; a CPA license; her parents' dignity intact.
**Fears.** That her precision is the same appetite she hates, wearing glasses.
**Weaknesses.** Justice as a project plan; can't receive kindness without auditing it.
**Money.** Bar shifts at Halcyon from C3; the family resettled in New Jersey; she stays.
**With the protagonist.** The receipts she keeps (the C2 meeting where Leo pulled the rug) are a loaded gun on the wall of the whole story. → doc 07 §2.14, doc 08 §3.

### 2.15 Rachel Voss — 28 · scenic artist, Inwood

**Background.** Ohio college with Leo (Y-6); left him in Y-3 February; moved to New York a year later on her own plan, painting sets off a Broadway-adjacent scene shop and teaching herself welding.
**Personality.** Unimpressed is her resting face; exact where Leo is fluid. Kept every version of the person he was, which is why his new one doesn't scan.
**Wants.** Her own scene shop by 35; one friendship per year that survives the year; to never again be someone's white whale of self-improvement.
**Fears.** That he was right about her leaving — that she chose the exit over the person.
**Weaknesses.** Finishes his sentences in her head; answers his texts at 1 a.m. every single time.
**Secrets.** The payphone call (doc 02 F5) — she took it, at the scene shop's desk, and told no one, because what he said wasn't hers to carry and she carried it for years anyway.
**With the protagonist.** Enters in C4 at a gallery thing Fitz drags him to. She is the one person who cannot be managed, impressed, or re-narrated. → doc 07 §2.15.

### 2.16 Camille Odom — 33 · teacher, P.S. 218

**Background.** Fifth-grade teacher, ten years; Georgia roots, Harlem born; the quiet "alternative life" romance if the player pursues it (C3–C4). Runs the school's food pantry the way Yoli runs the garden: logistics as love.
**Personality.** Steady, warm, allergic to being anyone's award. Values a Tuesday that repeats.
**Wants.** A partner whose five-year plan has a Saturday in it; the pantry funded past June.
**Fears.** Mistaking momentum for love.
**Weaknesses.** Gives three chances; the third is never earned back.
**Secrets.** Turned down a district job to stay in the classroom; tells no one because it sounds like a brag.
**With the protagonist.** The exit on her terms in C4 (doc 06): "You talk about people like they're pieces on a board. I'm on your board." — doc 07 §2.16.

## 3. Supporting cast (one breath each; full behavior notes in doc 07 §6)

- **Tito Ferreira, 66** — retired MTA track worker; the dominoes senate; knows where 1994 is buried and pays the rent on the silence. Two lines a chapter, both load-bearing.
- **Miss Odessa Wynn, 71** — raises her grandson Eli above the Cecil's teal door; the gold window's keeper; invests $800 of a funeral-insurance tin in the Club (doc 09). The story's most expensive kindness.
- **Eli Wynn, 9** — the kid from the gold window ("whatever's cold"). Notices everything, forgets nothing, saves a stool.
- **Big Mike, 58** — the Alder Diner on Sycamore; Greek-American; the 50th-anniversary cookbook arc survives as the supporting storyline it always was; invests $5,000 in the Club because food halls are his love language.
- **Deon King, 47** — Fade King barbershop; the wire service; refuses the first market-rate offer in C4 and becomes the block's proof of concept for holding on.
- **Fatima Rahal, 39** — Gable Row 3; catering and alterations from home; the Gable Garden's co-founder; the coalition's supply line.
- **Grace & Raymond Okafor, 49 & 55** — home health aide and MTA trackman; Kev's parents; the Gable Garden's keepers; Grace invests $1,500 of cleaning money. The family the Compact was supposed to protect in 1994.
- **Fitz, 30** — Halcyon's co-owner; front-of-house velvet; the one who drags Leo to the gallery where Rachel is; invests $4,000 with his eyes open.
- **Father Mike Brennan, 58** — Ascension on Sycamore; good man, tired boiler; invests the parish's **boiler fund** ($2,500) against his own better judgment — the sin he'll be doing penance for in doc 09.
- **Imam Rahim Dawan, 47** — Masjid an-Nur; Idris's five a.m.; refuses the Club politely and never mentions it again after C5, which is its own rebuke.
- **PO Diane Mercado, 36** — the plaza's beat cop; decent, limited, keeps the terminal's overnighters from being "moved along" harder than the city asks.
- **Marcus Bell, 40** — parks-department youth coach at Marlow Playground; the adult in every room with a whistle.
- **Dr. Naomi Feld, 51** — Leo's therapist from C4; office two stops up the K. Says less per session than anyone in the cast; means all of it. Her canon lines are registered in doc 15.
- **Eleanor "El" Vann, 81** — retired city planner; two bedrooms of the East Tenement are paper — the 1994 Compact's surviving file. The story's librarian of buried leverage.
- **Verna Carter, 58** — Deshaun's mother; school crossing guard; the $2,300 investor who never once makes Leo feel it, which is worse.
- **Ada, Gerry & Bea Marsh, 59, 65, 31** — Elyria, Ohio. Ada (hospital billing) sends the **$8,000** in C3 and asks zero questions, which is how love sounds on a bad phone line. Gerry's aphasia rehab is its own quiet war. Bea (nurse, Columbus) manages the family's truth and will not manage Leo's career.
- **Council Member Alma Reyes, 61** and **Kayla Fournier, 44** — the district's machine and its hands; competent, electoral, survivable.
- **Lena, 34 & Odd, 28** — the muralists (LMK-05, day-keyed progress). In C5 they repaint the "LIAR" tag into something the block chooses together.
- **Sam, 24** — the Day 4 stranger with the duffel (shipped beat, doc 11); becomes Corner Slice's weekend hire in C1; exits to a union apprenticeship in C3 — the block's hospitality law, working.
- **"Anton," voice only** — the Ferrante family's telephone manner. Never seen. Two calls in the whole story, both quotations of kindness.
- **Mango** — the bodega cat. Canon immune. Nothing bad ever happens to Mango. This is a design law.
