## The one-line version

A man with holes in his memory arrives in a neighborhood that keeps a diary of everyone, becomes somebody by borrowing against everyone, is betrayed by the record he kept, pays for it without going to prison, and spends the rest of the game deciding — in ink, in installments, in public — what he is for. The city keeps moving. The block keeps a light on. **The game does not end at the fall.**
