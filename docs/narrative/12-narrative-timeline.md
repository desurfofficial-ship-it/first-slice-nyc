## 3. The 1994 Compact (full backstory, spoiler-graded)

The Alder Works parcel, the air rights, and a community land trust proposal — one vote away in the spring of '94. Hartwell Corp's counter-offer was better money *and* a villain the neighborhood could see. Ernie Ferreira, shop steward, the block's strongest voice, delivered 214 signatures against the CLT — nine Gable Row families among them — and took a consulting position at Hartwell in June. The vote died. The crash of 2008–2011 took Hartwell; the option lapsed unexercised; title tangled. The papers survived in El Vann's files: the CLT bylaws, the CBA draft, the correspondence — and, separately, in Tito's dresser, Ernie's letter ("*The block was going to lose anyway. I saved what I could.*"), written Y-17 and read once. **No conspiracy:** a development economics problem, a family wound, and municipal dust. The C6 concessions are won from this paper by Yoli — the block out-organizing its ghost.

## 5. Season canon (art note)

The shipped build's "October, golden hour, forever" is C0's mood-lock. From C1, seasons advance with the calendar: the nor'easter (late Oct Y1), first snow (C2's opening week — the GDD's First Snow beat, absorbed), summer (C3), a second winter (C4), the hot late summer of the fall (C5), snow on the hoarding (C6's open). The seasonal pass is a builder dependency (doc 15).

## 6. In-game time model (summary; spec in doc 14 R4)

One in-game day ≈ 5–8 real minutes at the loop's default; free-roam and authored beats both advance the clock; **no real-world dates exist anywhere in the game**; chapter boundaries use diegetic season cuts; a full main spine ≈ 27 in-game months plus an open endgame year. Missed windows close; independent events fire; the calendar never negotiates.
