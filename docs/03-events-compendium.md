# FIRST SLICE: A New York Story
## Document 03 — NYC Events & Systems Compendium

*Every event obeys the Compendium Law: **magical, not overwhelming** — one wonder beat per event, zero punishment states, and at least one screenshot. Events are delivered by the city like gifts, never scheduled like chores. Durations: most events live inside one 5–8 minute day; "seasonal" events anchor a week.*

---

## Tier 1 — Signature Moments (the viral canon)

### 1. The Pizza Rat Moment 🐀🍕
**Trigger:** Rare, unscripted-feeling, ~once per in-game month at a subway platform.
A slice slips off your tray. A small, magnificent rat assesses it, grips it with both paws, and drags it down the platform with absolute purpose. Every phone in the crowd comes out. A kid narrates like a sportscaster.
**Wonder beat:** The rat pauses mid-platform, looks at you, and *nods*.
**Postcard:** *"You witnessed New York history."*
**Viral role:** The game's meme crown jewel — recreated, captioned, argued about.

### 2. The Bodega Cat 🐈
**Trigger:** Week 2, after your first regular unlock. The kitten adopts YOU (you don't choose it; the cat chooses you — this is correct).
Name it once; the name lives on receipts, postcards, and dialogue forever. Naps on the counter during rush, "helps" restocking by sitting in the box, judges new menu items with a single slow blink.
**Personality meter (hidden):** shaped by your choices — feeds at dawn = dignified; fed table scraps = a little gremlin; named something grand = acts grand.
**Wonder beat:** On hard days, the cat sits closer.
**Viral role:** Merch, stickers, "what did you name your cat" engagement bait.

### 3. Manhattanhenge 🌅
**Trigger:** Twice per in-game year, telegraphed 3 days out by the whole city getting excited.
The sunset aligns perfectly with the street grid. The entire city turns amber. Everyone — Regulars, strangers, the cat — walks to the middle of the avenue (it's a game; it's safe) and just… looks up.
**Wonder beat:** For 20 seconds, every NPC stops moving and faces the sun. The whole diorama holds its breath.
**Postcard:** *"The city stopped. You were there."*

### 4. First Snow ❄️
**Trigger:** First in-game winter, then annually with new layers.
Overnight, the entire map transforms: muffled audio, glowing windows, the cart wears a little snow hat. Snow-day rush for cocoa. Rosa sends soup. Idris's order becomes "two sugars… and something warm."
**Wonder beat:** The first snowflake lands on your character's nose and they *cross their eyes* to look at it.
**Postcard:** *"First snow. The whole city got quieter and kinder at the same time."*

### 5. The Broadway Lottery 🎭
**Trigger:** Daily tiny chance, forever; guaranteed win once per story arc.
Win = full evening cutscene: the show rendered entirely in silhouette against a glowing stage, original music, the audience around you laughing at moments you don't quite catch — which is exactly what it's like.
**Wonder beat:** During the silhouette scene, the silhouette of *you* and Priya (your +1) wipes a tear in perfect sync.
**Postcard:** *"Tonight, New York sang."*

## Tier 2 — Street Life & Seasons

### 6. Hydrant Summer 💦
Heatwave. A neighbor asks if you'll help open the hydrant — properly, with a city spray cap (a real NYC ritual the game teaches with love). Kids scream through the arc of water; your cart's foot traffic triples; the whole block smells like sunscreen and popsicles.
**Wonder beat:** A dog joins the kids, wearing sunglasses. No explanation ever.
**Postcard:** *"96°F. The block found a way."*

### 7. Parade Days 🎈
- **The Giant Balloon Parade:** balloons float *over your street* at rooftop height; you serve hot chocolate to rope-holders
- **Lunar New Year:** Mrs. Chen's dragon runs the full length of Chinatown; the whole cast shows up
- **Pride:** your block paints its windows; Regulars march; the cat wears a tiny bandana
- **Dominican Day / Carnival:** uptown turns into a moving concert; Samir closes his cart to dance
**Wonder beat:** Each parade ends with one balloon/dragon/confetti frame designed to be THE screenshot of the year.

### 8. Subway Buskers & Showcase Nights 🎻
Buskers appear on platforms (ambient charm). Once your spot grows, you can **host a busker** — their music becomes your shop's soundtrack, crowds gather, some become Regulars. Season highlight: the **Rooftop Showcase**, where every musician you've met plays one song as the sun sets.
**Wonder beat:** The final Showcase song is played by *everyone at once*, and the skyline lights up like it's listening.

### 9. Stoop Sale Saturdays 🪑
Weekend mornings: stoop sales bloom. Buy weird, wonderful junk — a chipped mug ("the mug Idris refuses to replace"), a lamp shaped like the Empire State, a box of someone's grandfather's baseball cards. Most items become shop décor **with their stories attached** — visitors to your shop can tap them.
**Wonder beat:** One sale per season is a mystery — the stoop of a retired magician. Everything is slightly magic.

### 10. Late-Night Slice Runs 🌙
A Regular texts at 2 AM: *"You up? Slice run."* The city is empty, glowing, and quiet — the art team's showcase hours. These walks carry the game's deepest conversations: Samir on feeding a city that's asleep, Lena on why she stays, Idris — once, only once — on his late wife.
**Wonder beat:** The slice shop window glows like a lantern in the dark. It is always there. It will always be there.
**Postcard:** *"2 AM. The city talks softest after midnight."*

### 11. Game Days ⚾🏀
Yankees win → the whole city's dialogue pool changes for a day; a stranger high-fives you on the street (a one-button interaction that feels *great*). Knicks playoffs → every TV in every bodega is on; nobody minds that you're watching instead of shopping. Endgame: you host the **Rooftop Watch Party**.
**Wonder beat:** The city-wide high five — one frame, 50 NPCs, one motion.

### 12. Rain Days ☔
Rain makes the city *more* beautiful here: wet cobblestones double every light. Umbrella-borrowing minigame; the delivery rush becomes a cozy puzzle ("soup weather" = soup sells out).
**Wonder beat:** Two strangers share one umbrella under your awning and become, over three rainstorms, a thing.
**Postcard:** *"Rain in New York is just the city putting its lights on twice."*

## Tier 3 — Social & Story Systems

### 13. The "I Got You" System 🤝
Small kindnesses — carrying a stroller up subway stairs, giving directions you only know because the game taught you, the busker's coin — feed an invisible city-karma pool. **No meter is ever shown.** It returns as luck: the day you're short on stock, a driver "happens" to have extras; the day the cat is sick, the vet "happens" to owe Rosa a favor.
**Design law:** the game never confirms causality. Players *believe*, and that belief is the mechanic.
**Wonder beat:** The first time karma returns, the game gives no explanation whatsoever. It just… goes well.

### 14. The Welcome Committees 🗽
Unlocking a new borough triggers a Welcome Quest hosted by a local:
- **Brooklyn** — Fatima's stoop-sale treasure hunt (ends on the bridge at sunset)
- **Queens** — Big Mike's World Food Crawl (seven flags, seven dishes, one afternoon)
- **The Bronx** — José's block party (you cater it; it's the first event where *you* host *the neighborhood*)
- **Staten Island** — Eddie's last route: the game's quietest, kindest hour, ending on the ferry deck at golden hour
**Wonder beat:** Each borough welcome ends with a local handing you something small — a bagel, a metrocard-shaped keychain, a dumpling, a foam finger, a ferry token — the game's version of a house key.

### 15. The Happy Accident System 🧭
Getting lost never costs anything — it *finds*. Hidden spots (a community garden, a record shop, the best slice you've ever had) are only discoverable off-route. The loading screen states the philosophy once: *"In New York, getting lost is just finding somewhere new."*

### 16. The Key Jingle & City Soundtrack 🎵
Dawn = keys. The game's most Pavlovian comfort cue: keys jingling means the city is opening up, the day is beginning, the regulars are coming. Night = the rumble under everything. The city has a heartbeat and it's a bass line.

### 17. Multilingual Chatter 🗣️
Ambient street conversations drift past in Spanish, Mandarin, Korean, Bengali, ASL, French, Yoruba. Eavesdropping (standing still near chatter) occasionally rewards a **slang card** or a **story snippet** — the game teaches that the city is a world, quietly.

### 18. Steam, Pigeons & the Rule of Motion 💨
Always-on ambient systems: manhole steam on cold mornings, pigeons with opinions, paper bags tumbling in that one specific way. The frame is never dead. (Art law: the Rule of Steam — see Doc 04.)

### 19. Mabel's Album 🎤 *(Regular arc showcase)*
Across three in-game months you help jazz singer Mabel record her first album — finding the studio, the drummer, the courage. The finale is the **Listening Party** on your rooftop: her voice over the skyline, every Regular silent, the cat asleep on the amp.
**Wonder beat:** The last note holds exactly as long as the camera holds the skyline.
**Postcard:** *"Harlem's newest record. You were there when it started."*

### 20. Rosa's Bodega Anniversary 🎉 *(Regular arc showcase)*
Rosa's 30th year. The whole block conspires to decorate overnight (a mission chain where you sneak past "sleeping" Rosa — she is awake the entire time and knows everything). At the party, she gives a speech she pretends not to have prepared.
**Wonder beat:** The bodega's awning is replaced overnight with one that reads: *"ROSA'S — 30 YEARS. STILL THE MOST IMPORTANT ONE."* (A callback to your first fun-fact sticker.)
**Viral role:** The "I cried at a bodega party and I've never been to New York" post.

### 21. The Block Party 🏮 *(endgame finale)*
The city closes YOUR street. Every Regular, every busker, every parade float you've ever met. Idris orders "the usual" one more time. Rosa hands you a small, cold thing: a key.
> *"You kept the door open. Now it's yours."*
**Postcard:** *"Day 365. You came here knowing nobody. Look around."*

## Event Cadence Rules

1. **One event per session, maximum** — wonder is a spice, not a meal.
2. **Signature moments are rare by design** — scarcity is what makes them post.
3. **Every event ends in warmth** — never a cliffhanger, never FOMO. The city is generous.
4. **Every event is a postcard** — if it can't be composed into one, it isn't an event yet.
